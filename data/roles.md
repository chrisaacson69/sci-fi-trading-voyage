# Roles — what each hull specialises in

> Chris, 2026-10-17: *"the whole point of this tool is to make less generalizations 'this ship is the
> best ever!' and more into what it specializes in."*

Every ranking in `data/hull_ceiling.csv` collapses a hull to one number against one pirate template,
and a week of comparisons has shown that number change its mind the moment armour, battle row or
target class changes. This page holds the descriptors that actually decide where a hull belongs.
Generated by `lagrange-combat/tools/roles.py` into `data/roles.csv` (144 hulls, each at its own full
ladder); the 26,732-row head-to-head grid is `lagrange-combat/matrix.csv`.

## The descriptors

| field | what it decides |
|---|---|
| **row** | `Tb_cfg_ship.ROW_INDEX` - 0 front, 1 mid, 2 rear. 36 / 77 / 51 hulls. A mid-row hull is screened: battle7 measured the row-1 Reliat A taking **zero** damage while the row-0 Eris HC took 1,543 per ship. Defensive stats on a back-row hull are worth only what the chance of the front collapsing makes them worth. |
| **armour_floor** | Where its biggest ballistic gun stops scaling. Ballistic damage floors at 10% of table damage, so a 93.8-damage torpedo adds nothing past armour 84. **The single most predictive number here.** |
| **keeps** | DPM at armour 240 as a share of DPM at 0. |
| **energy_pct** | Share of damage from Energy mounts, which ignore armour entirely. |
| **reach** | Carries a torpedo (weapon type 6). Chris: torpedoes *"can hit behind the front row esp if there is nothing in the front row that screens the weapon priority"*. |
| **dpm_cp / ehp_cp** | Where its BV comes from. A hull leaning on hit points is worth less in a fight you are winning, because the survivability is never cashed. |
| **repair** | Hit points healed per minute, directly comparable to incoming DPM. |

## The roles Chris named, with the numbers attached

| hull | CP | row | DPM/CP | EHP/CP | floor | keeps | energy | role |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Quaoar Torpedo** | 6 | 1 | 727 | 7,966 | 378 | 34% | 0% | **reaches past screens** - torpedoes hit the second row when nothing screens the priority |
| **Eris Heavy Cannon** | 9 | **0** | 406 | 8,937 | 324 | 28% | 0% | **front line**, wants healers behind it |
| **Taurus Defense** | 8 | **0** | 301 | 9,362 | - | **100%** | **100%** | **armour-proof** - all energy, immune to the armour cliff |
| **Reliat Stealth** | 4 | 1 | 393 | **23,672** | 216 | 8% | 0% | **fire sponge** - bolt onto a CA fleet to attract fire |
| **Carilion Special** | 5 | **0** | **21** | **13,102** | 32 | 9% | 0% | **evasion tank** - absorbs, contributes almost nothing |
| **Reliat A** | 4 | 1 | 104 | 6,156 | **81** | 8% | 0% | **anti-FF/DD only** |

### Two places the data refines the list

**The Carilion Special is the tank; the Recon is not.** Special 13,102 EHP/CP against Recon's 5,534 -
and the Special does 21 DPM/CP, so it is almost purely a sponge. battle7 bears it out: the Carilion
Recon dealt 6,007 and took 11,132, the only hull in that fleet absorbing more than it contributed.

**The Reliat Stealth's sponge role is narrower than it looks.** Its 23,672 EHP/CP is nine times its
raw hit points, entirely from evasion - but it floors at armour 216 and keeps only **8%** of its
damage at 240. It attracts fire well and contributes little once the enemy has armour, and it sits in
row 1 where the fire may never arrive. Its #1 frigate ranking is an even-fight figure.

## The Reliat A falloff, measured

Chris: *"That reliat A is going to fall off hard at cruisers and higher, but right now, it does lots
of work vs FF and DD."* With his real 51 TP build, against each class at that class's median armour:

| target | median armour | DPM | share of its anti-FF output |
|---|---|---|---|
| FF | 5 | 5,193 | 100% |
| DD | 20 | 4,148 | **80%** |
| CA | 50 | 2,671 | 51% |
| BC | 180 | 487 | **9%** |
| CV | 112 | 487 | 9% |

Not a gentle taper - a cliff at armour 84, past which it contributes 487 DPM and nothing more. No
amount of tech points moves that; `103`/`104` at L5 raised the torpedo from 75 to 93.8 damage and
pushed the floor from 68 to 84, which widens the useful band slightly and does not change the shape.

## What this page cannot tell you

Roles are per-hull; fights are sequential. Which hull screens which, whether a weapon's priority table
lets it reach past the front, and whether the healers out-heal the incoming before the front collapses
are all fleet-level and none of them are modelled. Chris: *"in a perfect world, we would just model
combat as best as possible and monte-carlo fleets."*

Two inputs that would need are now in place and were not a week ago: the hit tables carry a
**`priority`** per target class alongside the hit rate (the mechanism behind the torpedo reach, read
by nothing yet), and `matrix.repair_pm` gives healing in the same units as incoming DPM, so "can the
healers hold the front" is arithmetic.

Craft are not covered here - `roles.py` walks `playable_weapons`, which excludes fighters and
corvettes. Their armour curves are in `data/upgrades.md`.

## Counters: ship-to-ship beats the average (2026-10-18)

Chris: *"if we are going ship to ship, we can model ship to ship instead of using averages... if a
ship has high evasion, is there something out there that counters it?"*

Yes, and the averaged effective-HP figure actively hides it.

### Evasion is per ATTACKER WEAPON TYPE, and one type is always unprotected

A maxed Reliat Stealth buys `EFFECT_AVOID_INC_BY_WEAPON_TYPE` on top of its 70% base:

| attacker weapon type | total evasion | shots that land |
|---|---|---|
| **2 railgun** | **70%** | **30%** |
| 1 artillery / 3 ion / 4 pulse | 85% | 15% |
| 5 missile | 102% | 0% |
| 6 torpedo | 110% | 0% |

It buys +32 against missiles, +40 against torpedoes, +15 against artillery, ion and pulse - **and
nothing at all against railguns.** So a railgun lands twice as often as anything else and is the hard
counter. Per-attacker, against every hull in the game, the spread is **29% at best, 13% median, 0% at
worst** - so `roles.csv`'s single EHP figure of 8.99x raw hit points is an artefact of averaging over
one pirate team. Against a railgun it is nearer 3.4x; against a pure-missile hull it is unkillable.

Best counters are cheap: the **Quaoar Railgun** at 6 CP lands 29% and puts 1,047 DPM on it, and the
Alkaid-class pair are pure railgun at 6 CP. The hulls that cannot touch it at all are pure missile -
Arctos B and C, Alioth-class Type B, Mare Nubium Siege, Guardian Amphibious - which makes **an
all-missile fleet a composition trap** rather than a hull problem.

### The duel grid had to be regenerated, and needed a maxed mode

Chris's other concern: *"my only concern was if the matrix and grid got updated as we added
mechanics."* `matrix.csv` was dated **30 September** and predated every fix of the last week - flat
damage adds, ship-level hit points, module hit points, repair, strategies, the fight-length cap. It is
regenerated.

More important: the grid was **stock-vs-stock**, and weapon-type evasion only exists as an UPGRADE. A
stock Reliat Stealth evades a flat 70% of everything and `ev_by_wtype` is empty, so no counter-play
was visible at all. `tools/matrix.py --max` now fields every hull fully upgraded and writes
`matrix_maxed.csv`. The mechanism then shows up as the thing that does NOT move:

| opponent weapon type | stock grid | maxed grid |
|---|---|---|
| 6 torpedo | 58% | **12%** |
| 1 artillery | 59% | 34% |
| 3 ion | 88% | 65% |
| 5 missile | 73% | 40% |
| **2 railgun** | **59%** | **59%** |

Every type's win rate against the Reliat Stealth falls when it maxes its evasion, except railguns,
which are unchanged because there is no railgun avoidance to buy. Ion's 65% is higher in absolute
terms only because ion-armed hulls are individually strong; the diagnostic is the delta.

**Read `matrix_maxed.csv` for fielded ships and `matrix.csv` for stock**, and regenerate both after any
mechanic change - they are snapshots, not live views, and the 30 September file was wrong for three
weeks without anyone noticing.
